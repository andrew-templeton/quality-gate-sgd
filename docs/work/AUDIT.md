## Remaining evidence and interpretation

The next work is to execute the frozen reader study with eligible people, retain missing/failed/inconclusive outcomes, assess comprehension and source preservation separately from preference, and record the observed display conditions and burden. A complete exploratory evaluation can report failure or uncertainty; a favorable result is not required. It must not be promoted into confirmatory qualification while its sampling and reference-label limitations remain.

The bundled baseline/candidate study can describe the whole contrast on this case. It cannot identify the separate contribution of each gate facet. Composition assessment must retain the component rejection cases and correlated failures as well as human outcomes, and distinguish observations from claims that require additional study designs.

After the required observations and analysis exist, review decision-density-disclosure, audience-calibration and composition-calibration in that order, then their public-end-to-end and full-scope dependents.
